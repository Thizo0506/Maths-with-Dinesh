Dinesh Jothirathna

Welcome to Maths with Dinesh Jothirathna.

This space is dedicated to sharing mathematics lessons, explanations, problems and useful learning content for students.

Dinesh Jothirathna is a Mathematics teacher with over 20 years of teaching experience. He currently teaches Mathematics at Weragoda Vijayaba Secondary School and previously taught at G/P. De S. Kularathna College, Ambalangoda.

He teaches Mathematics for students from Grades 6–11 and also conducts private physical classes and home teaching for students.

He graduated from Siyane National College of Education and is dedicated to helping students understand Mathematics through clear and simple explanations.
